soa-tables - from the printed table to schemas

A Schedule of Activities table is a 2D presentation of study logic, constrained by paper. Footnotes are the overflow for logic that does not fit the grid. soa-tables transcribes the table into five constructs, carries them through three schemas, and lets human judgement in only through sidecars.

The constructs of an SoA table

Every SoA table, whatever its layout, is read as the same five constructs. They are the blocks of the extraction schema.

How corrections work

Review finds things. A reviewer does not edit a file; the reviewer writes an entry in a sidecar, and a program applies it. There are two sidecars, one before Layer 2 and one before Layer 3.

The human is an adjudicator, not an editor. Judgement enters through an auditable sidecar, never by rewriting model output.
