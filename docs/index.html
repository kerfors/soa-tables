soa-tables

Schedule of Activities tables from clinical trial protocols, as data that can be checked against the printed page.

A Schedule of Activities table is a 2D presentation of study logic, constrained by paper. soa-tables transcribes each table into five constructs and carries them through three schemas. Every element points back to its place on the page, and human judgement enters only through sidecars.
